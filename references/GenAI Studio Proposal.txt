I propose a collaborative community vision website, in which generative AI modifies targeted satellite imagery based on community suggestions. The website’s base is a map of satellite imagery. Users select a section of imagery and type out their suggestion as to how they wish to improve the area. The AI, a visual-modification model such as Google Gemini’s Nano Banana, takes the user’s selected satellite imagery and modifies it to match the description. The resulting edit is overlaid on the satellite imagery basemap, outlined when the mouse hovers over to indicate an edit. When the edit is clicked on, a side menu appears adjacent, displaying the user prompt and its rating given by other residents in a manner similar to a social media post. Additionally, a text box is available for users to re-prompt and refine further: this calls the AI to modify the image further using an additional prompt. When this system of edits, edit scores, and edit refinements are utilized across dozens of iterations, the result is a patchwork of resident suggestions that form the basis for a new community comprehensive plan – a highly collaborative city vision in which ideas have been democratically envisioned, critiqued, and reimagined in real-time by all users.
